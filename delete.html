This page deletes the same things the app does, and it does not need the app installed.

This cannot be undone. Unused credits are not refunded.

Deleting your account removes:

  • Your profile, email address and credit balance
  • Every character you created, including its voice and its AI agent
  • Every scheduled call
  • Your call history and every memory summary

Any active subscription is cancelled first, so a deleted account cannot keep billing your card. Stripe keeps its own invoice records, which financial law requires it to retain and which we cannot erase on your behalf.
