Privacy policy

Last updated 18 September 2026

1. What this covers

CUE delivers AI-generated phone calls from characters you create. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and who it is sent to. It covers the CUE mobile app only.

2. Your account

We store your email address and an encrypted password so you can sign in, along with your credit balance and subscription status. CUE contains no advertising and no analytics trackers, and we do not sell personal data.

3. Characters you create

The name, description and backstory you write are stored on our servers so your characters work every time you open the app. If you set a character photo, only a reference to that image on your own device is stored. The photo itself is never uploaded.

4. Microphone and call audio

During a call your microphone audio is streamed in real time to ElevenLabs so the character can answer. The audio is processed live. CUE does not record or store it, and there is no way to play a call back. If you report a call, a written transcript of that call is stored with your report; see section 10.

5. Character memory

If you turn memory on for a character, a short written summary of the conversation is generated by Anthropic Claude and stored with that character so it can refer back to what you talked about. Turning memory off stops new summaries being made. Deleting the character deletes its summary.

6. Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. CUE never sees or stores your card number. Stripe keeps its own billing records, which we cannot delete on your behalf.

7. Who your data reaches

Supabase hosts the database and signs you in. ElevenLabs generates the live voice. Anthropic writes the memory summaries. Stripe processes payments. Each one receives only what it needs for that job, and none of them receive your data for advertising.

8. Deleting your account

Account, then Delete account, removes your profile, characters, schedules, call history and memory summaries, cancels any active subscription, and releases the voices your characters used. You can do the same thing without the app installed, at our account deletion page. This cannot be undone. Stripe keeps invoice records where financial law requires it.

9. Questions and changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. For questions or requests about your data, contact us at the support address shown on the CUE listing in the Google Play Store.

10. Reporting AI content

Characters are generated by an AI model, and you can report anything one of them says. Use Account, then Report AI content, or the Report this call button on the screen shown when a call ends. A report stores the reason you chose, any note you write, and a written transcript of the reported call, so that a person can review what was actually said. Reports are read by us and are not shown to other users. Deleting your account deletes your reports.
