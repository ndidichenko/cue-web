Privacy policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

1. What this covers

CUE delivers AI-generated phone calls from characters you create. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and who it is sent to. It covers the CUE mobile app only.

2. Your account

We store your email address and an encrypted password so you can sign in, along with your credit balance and subscription status. CUE contains no advertising and no analytics trackers, and we do not sell personal data.

3. Characters you create

The name, description and backstory you write are stored on our servers so your characters work every time you open the app. If you set a character photo, only a reference to that image on your own device is stored. The photo itself is never uploaded.

4. Call recordings

Every call is recorded. During a call your microphone audio is streamed to ElevenLabs, which turns your speech into text, has an AI model write the character's replies, and speaks them in the character's voice. ElevenLabs keeps an audio recording and a written transcript of each call on our behalf for 90 days, then deletes them automatically. We use the transcript to build character memory (section 5) and to review a call you report (section 10), and the recording to look into problems with a call. Recordings are never used for advertising, and no other user can hear or read them. Deleting a character deletes the recordings of its calls.

5. Character memory

If you turn memory on for a character, Anthropic Claude reads the transcript after each call and updates a short set of written notes about what you talked about, building on the notes from earlier calls. The notes are stored with that character, and the character reads them at the start of the next call. Turning memory off stops new notes being made. Deleting the character deletes its notes.

6. Payments

Credit packs are bought through the App Store or Google Play, which take the payment. CUE never sees your card number or payment details. Apple or Google send us a confirmation of each purchase, and we keep the parts we need to credit it exactly once: the store's transaction number, the pack and the date. Apple and Google keep their own billing records, which we cannot delete on your behalf.

7. Who your data reaches

Supabase hosts the database and signs you in. ElevenLabs runs the call itself: it turns speech into text, produces the character's voice, and keeps the recordings described in section 4. The character's replies are written by an AI model from Anthropic (Claude), which ElevenLabs runs for us, and Anthropic also writes the memory notes. Apple and Google process payments for purchases made in the app. Each one receives only what it needs for that job, and none of them receive your data for advertising.

8. Deleting your account

Account, then Delete account, removes your profile, characters, schedules, call history, call recordings and transcripts, and memory notes, cancels any active subscription, and releases the voices your characters used. You can do the same thing without the app installed, at our account deletion page. This cannot be undone. We keep the purchase records described in section 6, with nothing that links them to you, so that a purchase can never be credited twice. Apple and Google keep their own billing records.

9. Questions and changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. For questions or requests about your data, contact us at the support address shown on the CUE listing in the Google Play Store.

10. Reporting AI content

Characters are generated by an AI model, and you can report anything one of them says. Use Account, then Report AI content, or the Report this call button on the screen shown when a call ends. A report stores the reason you chose, any note you write, and a written transcript of the reported call, so that a person can review what was actually said. Reports are read by us and are not shown to other users. Deleting your account deletes your reports.
