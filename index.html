About CUE

CUE is an Android app that delivers realistic incoming phone calls from AI characters you create yourself. You write who they are and how they sound, then they call you: straight away, on a schedule, or with one tap when you need a way out of a situation.

People use it as a believable excuse to leave somewhere uncomfortable, for company, and to practise difficult conversations before having them for real.

Your data and your account

CUE carries no advertising and no analytics trackers. Call audio is processed live and never recorded. Character photos never leave your phone. You can delete your account and everything in it at any time, from inside the app or from this site.
